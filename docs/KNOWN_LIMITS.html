Current limits — 2.4 RC3

  • Reference judgment timestamps and tolerance boxes are hypotheses. No temporal-grace or hard reachable-set solver, joint path/hand reoptimization, or full limb/block feasibility model is present.
  • Endpoints are relaxed using sampled interval containment; internal continuous contact is not certified. Solver success is numerical success.
  • Automatic outputs remain unreviewed. Green markers have several meanings depending on the view; they do not alone prove a third contact is required.
  • Frequency Peak remains the existing window estimator. Metrics are uncalibrated attributes, not official chart ratings. No local training occurs automatically.
  • Recognition uses its own fixed distance weighting. Changing a difficulty formula does not retrain or rerun allocation.
  • Files span workspace and external chart directories, with absolute pointers. Relocation/restore wizard and full project backup format are absent.
  • Structural editing can leave allocator-derived annotations stale. Re-evaluate those annotations explicitly.
  • Batch work can fail per stage; inspect logs and export error fields. Stop takes effect after the current chart.
  • Known unsupported group attributes are removed from generated previews, affecting decorative effects. Other game-format extensions can still require work.
  • Native Windows/Tk interaction and ArcCreate acceptance remain limited; unit tests and headless integration do not replace those checks.
